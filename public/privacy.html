Privacy policy

Last updated:

The Sunken Covenant is a free browser game. This page says what information the game handles, why, where it is kept and for how long, and what you can do about it. The game shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking and sets no cookies.

Stored only in your browser

The game keeps some things in your own browser (local storage and IndexedDB), never sent anywhere: your settings (language, sound and volumes, 3D quality, pointer, colour-blind colours), whether you have seen the tutorial, your last captain's name and commander, a battle in progress so you can return to it, and, on the sound desk page, the sounds you add there. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes them.

Who else handles it

The game runs on Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): Hosting delivers the pages, Authentication handles sign-in, and the Realtime Database stores online battles, accounts and error reports. The database is located in the European Union (Belgium, europe-west1); where Google's services process data outside the EU, it relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Google acts as a processor on the author's behalf. No data is sold or shared with anyone else. The game's type faces are served from this site itself.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to receive it in a portable form, to restrict its use or to object to it, by writing to . You can delete your account and its log yourself at any time from the Captain's Log. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

Children

Anyone may play. Captain's accounts are meant for those old enough to agree to this policy themselves under the law of their country (14 in Spain, 13 to 16 elsewhere in the EU); younger players should play without an account or with a parent's consent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.

El juego guarda algunas cosas en tu propio navegador (almacenamiento local e IndexedDB), sin enviarlas a ningún sitio: tus ajustes (idioma, sonido y volúmenes, calidad 3D, puntero, colores para daltónicos), si has visto el tutorial, tu último nombre de capitán y comandante, una batalla en curso para poder volver a ella y, en la página de sonidos, los sonidos que añadas allí. Quién más los trata

El juego funciona sobre Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): Hosting sirve las páginas, Authentication gestiona el acceso y Realtime Database guarda las batallas online, las cuentas y los informes de errores. La base de datos está en la Unión Europea (Bélgica, europe-west1); cuando los servicios de Google tratan datos fuera de la UE, se acogen a las cláusulas contractuales tipo de la Comisión Europea y al Marco de Privacidad de Datos UE-EE. UU. Google actúa como encargado del tratamiento por cuenta del autor. Los datos no se venden ni se ceden a nadie más. Las tipografías del juego se sirven desde este mismo sitio.
